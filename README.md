## 🔍 About

**Saudi Traffic Accidents Analysis** is a data science project aimed at studying and analyzing traffic accident statistics in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia between **2016 and 2019**. 

The project utilizes **Data Science** techniques to analyze a comprehensive dataset of accidents, injuries, and fatalities. It provides data visualizations that help in understanding the geographical distribution of accidents and temporal trends, ultimately contributing to the enhancement of road safety awareness.

---

## ✨ Features

- 📊 **Regional Analysis** — Detailed breakdown of accident distributions across different regions of the Kingdom.
- 📉 **Trend Modeling** — Tracking the evolution of fatalities and injuries over the years.
- 🎯 **Regression Insights** — Utilizing statistical regression lines to demonstrate the decline in mortality rates.
- 🗺️ **High-Impact Identification** — Identifying the most affected regions and areas with the highest traffic density.
- 📄 **Automated Reports** — Generating dynamic reports in PDF, Word, and HTML formats via R Markdown.

## 💡 Conclusion & Impact

The data clearly indicates a **continuous and significant decline** in the number of accidents and fatalities from 2016 to 2019 across all regions of the Kingdom. 

This positive trend is a direct result of the **evolution in traffic management and regulations** in Saudi Arabia, which includes:
1. **Automated Monitoring:** The expansion of automated traffic violation detection systems (e.g., Saher).
2. **Strict Law Enforcement:** Rigorous enforcement of traffic laws, such as mandatory seatbelt use and strict penalties for mobile phone usage while driving.
3. **Public Awareness:** Raising road safety awareness through continuous national campaigns.
4. **Infrastructure:** Improving road infrastructure and engineering to eliminate accident black spots.
